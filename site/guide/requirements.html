Requirements and traceability

What the scanner must do, how each requirement is verified, and where the evidence lives. Verification methods: Inspection, Analysis, Demonstration, Test. Evidence IDs point to VALIDATION.md: R-… are recorded checks a computer can run, T-… are physical tests.

Status is filled in by python scripts/gen_traceability.py from VALIDATION.md: Met (recorded) = all evidence is a passing recorded check; Open = at least one physical test has not been run yet. Values marked target are proposed goals for an educational instrument — confirm or change them after the first bench runs.
