Radar V6 "Mini" · open hardware

Three parts, seven wires — and you can see sound.

A desk-sized instrument that sweeps a tiny ultrasonic "two-eye" head on a servo and draws what it hears as a green radar-style picture on a 2.8" touch screen. One USB cable powers it. Every part, pin and number is documented.

Honest scope. This is ultrasonic sonar with a radar-style display — not radio-frequency radar, not a safety or obstacle-avoidance device. It sweeps about 120°, the angle shown is the commanded servo position, and nothing has been printed, fitted or powered yet. The pictures are renders.

Four printed parts, one CadQuery source

Every STL, STEP and 3MF is regenerated from cad/build_radar.py. No supports, no loose screws — press fits plus the two screws that come with the servo. Values that depend on your exact modules are marked VERIFY and checked with a printed fit coupon first.

  • 01Shell 104 × 96 × 62 mm — tilted screen opening, roof with turret hole and engraved angle scale, USB opening. Printed roof-down.
  • 02Bezel — the touch screen presses onto four pegs; slides into grooves in the shell.
  • 03Base — presses in and locks the bezel.
  • 04Head — the "two eyes": sensor cans press in; the servo horn presses into its foot.
  • 05fit coupon — print first (pegs, sensor holes, screw pilots)
