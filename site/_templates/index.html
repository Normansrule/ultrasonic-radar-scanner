Radar V5.1 · open hardware

Print it, wire it, flash it — and see sound.

A battery-powered desktop instrument that sweeps an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor on a servo and draws what it hears as a green radar-style picture. Every part, pin and number is documented so a first-time maker can build it and understand why it works.

Honest scope. This is ultrasonic sonar with a radar-style display — not radio-frequency radar, not a safety or obstacle-avoidance device. It sweeps about 120°, the angle shown is the commanded servo position, and nothing has been printed, fitted or powered yet.

Six printed parts, one CadQuery source

Every STL, STEP and 3MF is regenerated from cad/build_radar_v5.py. Each dimension is a named variable; anything that depends on your exact modules is marked VERIFY and checked with a printed fit coupon first.

  • 01Body 128 × 96 × 52 mm — electronics bay, side vents, rear USB-C opening, 45° self-supporting deck.
  • 02Front panel 118 × 52 × 2.6 mm — LCD window, 12 mm switch, corner locators.
  • 03Roof 120 × 74 × 3 mm — servo mount, vents, sensor-cable slot.
  • 04Rotor hub — carries the stock servo horn; keyed mast socket.
  • 05Turret keeper — anti-lift sleeve with engraved 30°–150° ticks.
  • 06Sensor head — open-backed HC-SR04 shroud and keyed mast, printed face-down.
  • 07optional 0.4 mm keeper shim · 08 fit-test coupon (print first)
